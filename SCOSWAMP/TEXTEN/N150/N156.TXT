T 156 Pile of Gravel

   Out of breath but alive, you see your adversary reduced to a pile of gravel
on the floor.

   You burst out laughing, throw Stratagus a mocking glance and toss the table
leg over your shoulder; it lands on the debris of a bookcase. Stratagus
contemplates the disorder that now reigns in the room.
