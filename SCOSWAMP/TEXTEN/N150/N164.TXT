T 164 The Bird Mistress

   The Copper Ring remains cold: the Bird Mistress is therefore benevolent.
You explain the purpose of your mission and she immediately begins hopping
with joy: "So there's still an Asphodel bush? What wonder!" Her enthusiasm is
so sincere that for a moment she resembles a delighted little girl.

   Aware of the importance of your quest for the cause of Good, she hands you
a magic potion: it has the power to restore to their initial value your SKILL,
STAMINA or LUCK points - your choice.

   Then, with a gesture, she calls several birds that come swirling around
her. She murmurs a few words to them, listens to them, then turns to you:
"Gayolard was right," she says softly, "there is indeed an Asphodel bush,
northeast of the Swamp. I can help you get there."

   You accept gratefully. Which characteristic do you restore?
