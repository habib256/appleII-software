The path northward gradually rises. The ground becomes drier and the swamp
vegetation gives way to a deep forest.

Soon, the trees thin out and you reach a vast clearing dotted with bushes.

You listen, but nothing abnormal disturbs the silence. Then, suddenly, two
enormous wolves appear and stare at you in the shadow.
