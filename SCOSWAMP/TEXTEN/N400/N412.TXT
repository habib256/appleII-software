T 412 The Square of Marshville
V 413

You leave Grognard to his tankard and push open the tavern door. Outside,
Marshville lies in the sun: a beaten-earth township set on the low lands, the
last one before the Swamp. Travellers pass through every day and nobody turns
to look at your steel helmet.

On the square, an old man dozes against his shop window. Further on, the
market is loud with shouting and poultry; a large house takes up one whole
side of it, its shutters closed. The street ends at the edge of the village,
among gardens. And to the north, where the ground turns soft, a black tower
stands out against the sky.

Three men of this village are looking for an adventurer. There is no hurry
yet: Grognard is waiting, and his tankard is full.
