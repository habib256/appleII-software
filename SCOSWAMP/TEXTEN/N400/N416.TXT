T 416 The End of the Village

The street stops at a row of gardens. In the last one a short, plump man in a
white tunic and linen trousers is turning a pot on his wheel: Gayolard, the
good sorcerer, whose door the whole village knows. He does not look up.

Turning round, you see the market and Pompatarte's large house, its shutters
closed against the noise of the stalls.

Then you look north. The ground turns soft there, the trees darken, and
Stratagus's tower rises close to the marsh, ringed with statues whose faces
are twisted into grimaces. You already know what your Ring will say when you
come near it.

None of the three can enter the Swamp himself. That is the whole problem, and
that is why they are looking for someone like you.
