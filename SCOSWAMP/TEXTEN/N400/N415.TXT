T 415 The Old Man's Shop

The old man opens one eye when your shadow crosses his window. He sells rope,
tallow and lanterns, and gives his advice away for nothing.

"Good Gayolard? Everyone here knows where he lives: the little house at the
end of the village, the one with a garden. A gentle man, who makes pottery.
Pompatarte is the large house beside the market; nobody knows much about him,
except that he is rich and that he laughs loudly. As for the tower, over there
to the north, we do not speak of it here."

He falls silent, and his eyes say the rest.
