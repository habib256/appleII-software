T 418 What Is Said of the Three Men

   All three have let it be known the same thing: they are looking for an
adventurer for the Swamp, and none of them can go there himself. For the rest,
the village talks.

   Gayolard, the good sorcerer, is looking for a plant. They call it the
Antherica; the forces of Evil have almost wiped it from the world and a single
bush is said to remain somewhere in the Swamp. He pays in magic, they say.

   Pompatarte, the red-skinned merchant, is looking for a road. His caravans
go around the Swamp and lose weeks doing it; a reliable map through to
Courbensaule would be worth a fortune to him, and a share of it to you.

   Stratagus, in his tower, is looking for silver Amulets, the ones the
sorcerers settled in the Swamp are said to wear about their necks. He pays in
gold, and pays well.

   Grognard will say only this: the first serves Good, the second is a
mystery, the third has put himself in the service of Evil.
