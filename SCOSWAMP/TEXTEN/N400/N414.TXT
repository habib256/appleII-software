T 414 The Taproom

   The room smells of beer and peat. They are still talking about you: a man
who wants to walk into the Swamp is not something you see every day. A boatman
tells you again that others tried before you and that their bones are
whitening at the bottom of the mud.

   The innkeeper, for his part, mostly remembers last week. A giant of a man
came and sat down here, two grey wolves at his heels, and he said almost
nothing. "One of the Masters," he whispers. "They have claimed the whole
territory for themselves. That one was not the sort to welcome visitors with
open arms."

   In his corner, Grognard watches you and says nothing.
