T 006 Gayolard's House

   You return to Gayolard's house. A fire crackles in the fireplace and a
delicious smell rises: some dish is simmering. The sorcerer welcomes you
cordially and looks at you carefully.

   He then asks you a simple and decisive question: "Do you have the berry?"
If it's the purple fruit of the Asphodel, everything changes.

   What do you do?

GU BA 175 Give him the Asphodel berry
CN BA 052 Admit that I did not bring back the berry
