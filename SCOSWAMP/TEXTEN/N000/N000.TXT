T 000 THE SCORPION SWAMP

A Gamebook Adventure

You are a seasoned adventurer seeking glory!

The Scorpion Swamp is a cursed place that no one dares explore. Monsters,
traps and evil sorcerers await you there. But legend speaks of a fabulous
treasure hidden in its depths...

Can you survive the perils of the Swamp and discover its secrets? Your choices
will determine your fate!

Commands:
 [SPACE/RETURN/ESC]  - Text, then HGR picture, then HGR + 4 text lines
 [A-Z]               - Make a choice
 [I] [H]             - The backpack, the help
 [Q]                 - Quit the adventure

C 001 Start the adventure
